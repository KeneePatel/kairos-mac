Kairos foundations

Native macOS 26. Liquid Glass only on navigation: sidebar, toolbar controls and the menu bar popover. Content sits on solid panels. One blue accent marks the primary action and the selection; every other colour carries a meaning, and every verdict pairs its colour with an icon and a word.
