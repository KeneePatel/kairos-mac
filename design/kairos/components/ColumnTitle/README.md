The list column's title: `page-title` in `ink` with a `callout` count line in `ink-secondary`, `space-7` from the top and sides. It replaces the window title and subtitle on list screens; the window keeps a short title for Mission Control.

- The count line says what the list holds now, in the person's terms: "247 to decide · 283 need a full read", "38 waiting · 2 in progress", "27 companies · 794 from the catalog".
- Provide: the title and the count line. Keep the line under 60 characters.
- Don't: put actions in the title row (the toolbar holds them), or a date unless the section is about a date.
