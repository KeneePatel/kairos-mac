A verdict as Kairos draws it: the icon and the word on a tinted capsule. Use it in rows, on the verdict card and in the menu bar tiles; large (28 px, `headline`) only on the pane's verdict card.

- Pairs: `yes` on `yes-bg`, `maybe` on `maybe-bg`, `no` on `no-bg`. Never swap the pairs or use the colour alone.
- Icons: `checkmark.circle`, `circle.lefthalf.filled`, `minus.circle` at the text's size.
- Provide: the verdict. The pill carries no action; the row or card does.
- Don't: tint a whole row with a verdict colour, add a fourth verdict, or drop the word in narrow columns (drop the icon first).
