A button whose shortcut is part of its label. Every decision on a job (Build, Save, Skip, Open posting, Mark applied) is one of these; the key cap is the same key the Job menu lists.

- One `accent` primary per view (white text 5.7:1). The rest take `control-fill` with `ink`. Plain buttons (`accent-text`, no fill) for links and undo.
- The key cap: `caption` on `surface-raised`, 1 px `outline`, `radius-keycap`, 16 px tall, at the trailing edge, 8 px from the label. On the primary button it reverses onto the accent.
- Height 28 px (medium control), capsule. Keep the label a verb and at most three words; the cost, when the action spends AI calls, goes in the label: "Re-screen · about 40 calls".
- Provide: the label, the key, whether primary, and the action. Hide the key cap from assistive tech; the control's shortcut carries it.
- Don't: show two primaries, put a key on a destructive button, or use a key cap as decoration where no shortcut exists.
