What a column shows when it has nothing: a `title2` title, one `body` line in `ink-secondary` that says what happens next or where things are, and at most one button. Centred in the column; no icon, no illustration.

- Titles are states, not apologies: "Nothing to decide", "No resumes yet", "No interviews yet", "Nothing filtered out".
- The line points at the next step: when Kairos checks again, where the jobs went, what to press.
- Provide: title, line, optional action. Don't: explain the feature, use more than two lines, or show an icon.
